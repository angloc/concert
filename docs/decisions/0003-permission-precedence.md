# 0003: Accent over singer over performance

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Date: 2026-11-26, from the register-and-compromise design note, marked "decided".

Compromises can be forbidden, permitted or mandated by the catalogue, the accent, the singer and the performance. When layers conflict, one must win.

## Decision

When permission layers conflict, the accent's setting stands, then the singer's. The performance decides only what the accent and the singer leave open.

## Consequences

Encapsulated by the permission resolver (not yet built). A careful singer cannot strip an accent's mandates, and a performance cannot override the singer.
